## The short version

My Diary does not collect, send, or sell any of your data. Everything you write stays on your
phone. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server. The one time anything
related to you leaves the phone is if you tap "Use current location" (see Location below).

## What the app stores

The app stores the following **only on your device**:

- Your diary entries (text, date, mood, and the place name if you add one)
- Photos you choose to attach, copied into the app's private storage
- Letters you write to your future self
- Your settings (appearance and app lock)

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup (see below). We (the
developer) cannot see, access, or recover it.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Photos:** only when you choose to attach a photo to an entry. The app reads only the photo
  you pick.
- **Face ID / fingerprint / passcode:** only if you turn on the app lock. Your phone's
  operating system handles this check, and the app never receives your biometric data. It only
  learns whether the unlock succeeded.
- **Notifications:** only to tell you on your phone when a sealed letter is ready to open.
  These notifications are scheduled on your device and are not sent through any server.
- **Location (approximate):** only when you tap **Use current location** while writing an
  entry. See below.

## Location

Adding a place to an entry is optional, and you can always type it yourself.

- The app uses your location **only when you tap "Use current location"**. It never asks for
  location when it starts or when you open an entry, never records your location
  automatically, and never uses location in the background.
- It asks only for approximate location (on Android, "approximate" rather than "precise").
- To turn your position into a neighbourhood name, the app uses the address service built
  into your phone's operating system. This means **your coordinates may be sent to Apple
  (on iPhone) or Google (on Android)**, under their own privacy policies. The app does not
  send them anywhere else.
- **The app does not store coordinates.** It only saves the place name you confirm (and can
  edit) with that entry, on your device and in any backup file you export.

## Backups

If you use **Save a backup**, the app creates a file with your entries (including any place
names), photos, and letters, including letters that are still sealed. You choose where to save or send it (for example,
your phone's Files app, a cloud drive, or email). That file is then handled by the service you
chose, under its own privacy policy. The backup file is not encrypted, so please keep it
somewhere private.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual entries and letters in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all of
its data from your device. Backup files you have saved elsewhere are not affected and need to
be deleted by you.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children,
because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds optional cloud sync, the new
version will be posted here with a new effective date, and the app will ask for your consent
before any data leaves your device.
