# App name ideas

The app's hook is **looking back**: memories that come back to you, and letters to your future
self. These names lean into that, rather than into "diary" (a very crowded word in both stores).

| Name | Why it fits |
|---|---|
| **Keepsake** | Something small you keep because it matters. Warm and grown-up. |
| **Afterglow** | The warm feeling of remembering a good day. |
| **Dear Later** | Echoes "Dear future me," the opening of every letter. |
| **Paper Days** | Matches the old-paper look of memories and letters. |
| **Yesteryou** | Playful: meet the you from a year ago. |
| **Tuck** | You tuck a day away and find it again later. Short and easy to remember. |
| **Letterbox** | Built around the letters feature, and a little nostalgic. |
| **Once Today** | Every day is "today" once, and then it's a memory. |
| **Rewind Journal** | Plain about what it does, which helps in search. |
| **Little Archive** | A personal archive of your life, without sounding heavy. |

## How to check a name before choosing

1. Search the **App Store** and **Google Play** for the name. Avoid names already used by
   another journal or diary app.
2. Search the name plus "app" on the web, and check for trademarks with
   [USPTO search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov) (US) or your own country's trademark office.
3. Check that a matching **app ID** is free to use, e.g. `com.stanley.keepsake`. Once
   published, the app ID can never change.
4. Store titles can include a short subtitle, e.g. "Keepsake: Diary & Memories", which helps
   people find the app by searching for "diary".

When you've picked one, the name goes in `app.json` (`name`, `slug`, `ios.bundleIdentifier`,
`android.package`), and then the icon and splash screen can be designed around it.
